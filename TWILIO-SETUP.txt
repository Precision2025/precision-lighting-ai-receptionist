TWILIO SETUP

1. Open Joshua's Twilio number.
2. Under Messaging Configuration, set “A MESSAGE COMES IN” to:
   https://YOUR-RENDER-DOMAIN/sms
   Method: HTTP POST
3. Confirm SERVICECHANNEL_VOICE_FROM is a voice-capable Twilio number.
4. Add SERVICECHANNEL-ENV.txt variables to Render.
5. Redeploy and test with a real tracking number.

INBOUND SMS / ADVANCED OPT-OUT
------------------------------
The /sms webhook now separates internal ServiceChannel commands from normal customer texts.

Messaging Service webhook:
https://precision-lighting-ai-receptionist.onrender.com/sms
Method: POST

In Twilio Console, open:
Messaging > Services > Precision Lighting Joshua SMS > Opt-Out Management

Set the HELP response to:
Precision Lighting: For assistance, call 855-533-4437 or reply to this message. Reply STOP to unsubscribe. Msg & data rates may apply.

Twilio may process HELP, STOP, and START before sending them to the webhook. The same HELP/START language is also implemented in server.js as a fallback.

Customer SMS routing:
- Accounting, billing, invoice, payment -> Accounting notification
- Job status, ETA, appointment, reschedule, service update -> Operations notification
- Travis, management, rentals, pool-light installation -> Management notification
- Other messages -> General office notification

Internal ServiceChannel commands remain restricted to SERVICECHANNEL_AUTHORIZED_NUMBERS.
